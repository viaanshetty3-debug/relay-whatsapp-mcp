01 · CHATGPT

Install your private Relay plugin

In ChatGPT Plugins, find the installed “Relay — Your AI workspace” plugin. Start a new conversation and enable it there. Use the same account on your other devices.

Open ChatGPT Plugins
02 · CLAUDE

Add a custom web connector

In Claude, open Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector. Use this server address, choose no OAuth sign-in, and add both private request headers below.

These credentials grant access to your WhatsApp tools. Enter them only in your own Claude connector’s request-header fields.

Open Claude connectors ↗
03 · CHECK THE CONNECTION

Try a harmless first request

Ask: “Check the WhatsApp bridge status and list my chats. Don’t send anything.” A successful tool call in each app confirms its connection.

On a new device, sign in to your authorized AI account and enable this connector. Account sign-in and security settings control device access. The Mac must remain awake and online.
